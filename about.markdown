## Why this site exists

This is a public notebook for ideas, technical notes, experiments, and occasional rants. It documents work in progress and the things learned along the way. Think of it as a spaza shop of thoughts: small, slightly unpredictable, but you might still find exactly what you came for.

The writing is not limited to one discipline. Some entries explore software engineering, data and privacy, telecoms, or Spring and messaging. Others are more reflective: notes about tools, machines, art, and the relationship between people and technology.

Topics may change direction faster than a taxi driver deciding to stop - the main point is still somewhere ahead, and the journey usually comes with a story or two.

## What you will find here

- **Writing**: longer notes and essays in the notebook on the home page.
- **Projects**: links to selected work and experiments.
- **Feel**: a small visual collection of images and ideas.
- **Scripts**: shortcuts and useful scripts hosted elsewhere.

The goal is to leave useful breadcrumbs, explain concepts clearly, and make room for questions that do not yet have tidy answers.

## How to read it

Entries are written as notes rather than polished presentations. They may evolve, be corrected, or grow as the understanding behind them changes.

> “Thuto ke lesedi.” — Education is light.
> “Go ithuta ga go fele.” — Learning never ends.
> “Monna o tee ga a tlhole naga.” — One person cannot manage a community alone.
